What are L3 Risks?

L3 Risk = the most granular, specific risk scenario within the risk hierarchy
Think of it as:


L1 Risk → Broad risk category
(e.g. Non-Financial Risk, Credit Risk)


L2 Risk → Sub-category / risk type
(e.g. Technology Risk, Regulatory Compliance Risk)


L3 Risk → Specific, actionable risk scenario
(e.g. “Failure to patch critical systems”, “Incorrect regulatory reporting”, “Payment processing failure due to interface error”)
