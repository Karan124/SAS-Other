Payments Risk Control Sufficiency PoC: approach and delivery plan

Hi all,

With the Payments Risk Controls Prioritisation deck endorsed, I want to set out how I'll deliver the work and what's different about the approach I'm taking.

What's different

The deck describes what good looks like. The runbook attached describes how we get there in twelve concrete steps, executable end to end with the data and tooling we have today. Three things in the approach are worth flagging because they change the shape of what we'll find:

  •  We don't trust the Payments Risk tag in JUNO alone. Payment controls hide under other risk libraries, under non Payments business units, and under legacy taxonomies. The approach actively finds them so the sufficiency answer is built on the full population, not the tagged population.
  •  We don't trust the value chain in Holocentric alone. Payment processes hide in non payments value chains for the same reasons. The approach finds those too.
  •  We include controls that operate in practice but aren't formally registered in JUNO. Process steps that perform a control function, system enforced controls in our applications, and remediation controls put in place after incidents. These become a registration backlog as a by product of the work.

What you'll get

  •  A view of where Payments Risk is and isn't sufficiently controlled, by Master Control, by Payment Nature, by stage of the payment
  •  A prioritised list of gaps with the type of gap clearly labelled (no control, weak control, control failing despite a strong rating, etc.)
  •  A duplicate controls register with a best of breed recommendation for each cluster
  •  A backlog of controls operating in practice that should be registered in JUNO
  •  A recommended monitoring baseline, with intensity proportional to inherent risk

Why this approach delivers faster and more credibly

The work is structured so that each step is independently checkable. Every AI classification cites the source text that justifies it. Items the AI can't classify with confidence are tagged for review rather than guessed at. SME validation will be sought where needed for edge cases. Nothing in the output is unsourced. A 3LoD reviewer can pick any cell in any output and trace it back to source data, the prompt that produced it, and the rationale. The full design is in the attached runbook.
