PAYMENT PROCESS RELEVANCE AND CATEGORY MAPPING - HOLISTIC PROMPT UPLIFT

PURPOSE
This guidance improves the prompt used by a Large Reasoning Model (LRM) to assess Holocentric process/activity data.

The model must perform two separate but related tasks for every process:
1. Determine whether the process is actually payment-related or payment-enabling.
2. If payment-related, map the process to one or more valid payment categories based on the ultimate payer-payee relationship.

The model must not force a payment category where the process is payment-related but payer-payee evidence is insufficient.

IDENTIFIER RULE
Each input process includes l3_process_UUID.
This is the unique process identifier and must be copied exactly into the output.
Do not use l3_activity_id or process_id as the unique join key because the same activity ID may appear in multiple process contexts.

VALID PAYMENT CATEGORIES
Use only the following categories:
- Customer to Customer
- Customer to Institution
- Institution to Customer
- Institution to Institution
- Supplier / Contractor / Employee Payments

CATEGORY DEFINITIONS
Customer to Customer:
Individual, merchant or business pays another individual, merchant or business.

Customer to Institution:
Customer pays the bank or deposits/repays funds into the institution.

Institution to Customer:
Bank or institution disburses or pays funds to the customer.

Institution to Institution:
One bank or financial institution pays, settles with, clears with, or exchanges obligations with another institution.

Supplier / Contractor / Employee Payments:
The bank pays suppliers, contractors or employees, including internal payroll and vendor payments.

CORE RULE 1 - PAYMENT LIFECYCLE VS PRODUCT / ACCOUNT / FACILITY / CONTRACT LIFECYCLE
An activity is payment-related only if it directly participates in the payment lifecycle.

Payment lifecycle activities include:
- receiving payment instructions
- validating payment instructions
- authorising payments
- executing payments
- routing payments
- clearing payments
- settling payments
- posting payment entries
- reconciling payments
- monitoring payment execution
- notifying payment status
- investigating payment exceptions
- recalling payments
- recovering payments
- controlling or governing payment execution
- operationally supporting payment execution or settlement

Do not classify an activity as payment-related merely because it is associated with a payment-enabled product, account, card, loan, deposit, facility or customer.

Exclude activities whose primary purpose is:
- account creation
- account setup
- account activation
- customer onboarding
- customer verification
- product implementation
- product configuration
- facility setup
- facility documentation
- contract preparation
- terms and conditions preparation
- financial table preparation
- customer communications
- account lifecycle management
- facility lifecycle management
- contract lifecycle management
- customer lifecycle management

unless the supplied evidence clearly shows direct payment execution, payment instruction handling, payment settlement, payment reconciliation, payment exception management or payment control.

CORE RULE 2 - ULTIMATE PAYER-PAYEE PRINCIPLE
Always determine the payment category from the ultimate payer and ultimate payee.

Do not determine category from:
- SWIFT
- RTGS
- RITS
- NPP
- BPAY
- Direct Entry
- Visa
- Mastercard
- PEXA
- OFI
- clearing
- settlement
- inbound
- outbound
- lifecycle stage
- activity title alone

Payment rails, channels and settlement mechanisms are execution context. They are not payment categories.

CORE RULE 3 - INBOUND / OUTBOUND WORDING
Inbound and outbound indicate payment direction only.
They do not identify the payer-payee category.

Determine category using evidence such as:
- payment purpose
- product or service
- borrower
- beneficiary
- merchant
- biller
- customer
- financial institution
- settlement counterparty
- account owner
- scheme participant
- clearing participant

CORE RULE 4 - CUSTOMER REPAYMENT RULE
Where a customer initiates, authorises or submits a payment to repay:
- loan
- mortgage
- credit card
- overdraft
- lease
- other obligation owed to a financial institution

classify as:
Customer to Institution

Do not classify as Institution to Institution solely because interbank clearing, RTGS, SWIFT, BPAY, Direct Entry, OFI processing or settlement occurs behind the scenes.

CORE RULE 5 - PAYMENT-RELATED BUT CATEGORY-UNCERTAIN IS ALLOWED
If the process clearly handles payment activity but does not contain enough evidence to determine the ultimate payer-payee relationship, return:

is_payment_process = true
mapped_categories = []
primary_category = null
category_confidence_score = Low

Do not force a category.

ADDITIONAL CONTEXT FIELDS
The payload may include these fields:

1. l3_activity_channels
This identifies channels or locations where the process takes place or originates, such as ATM, Branch, Relationship Manager, Digital Online/Mobile, Corporate Online or GTS Direct Connectivity.

2. l3_activity_customer_segments
This identifies customer or party segments associated with the process, such as Consumer, High Net Worth, Commercial, Small Business or Institutional.

3. l3_activity_product_service
This identifies the product or service associated with the activity, such as Investor Home Loans, Owner Occupied Home Loans, Transaction Accounts, Trade Finance, Asset Finance, Debt Products, Loan Products, Bonds / REPOs, FX Derivatives, Loans and Deposits, Structured Investments & Deposits or Structured Notes.

Use these fields as supporting evidence only.
They can help infer payment relevance, business context and likely payer-payee relationship.
They must not determine the category on their own.

PRODUCT / SERVICE CONTEXT RULE
The l3_activity_product_service field is a strong supporting signal because it often explains the business purpose of the payment.

Examples:
- Home Loans, Loan Products, Debt Products, Asset Finance or Credit Products may indicate Customer to Institution for repayments or Institution to Customer for disbursements.
- Transaction Accounts may indicate customer-originated payments, deposits or transfers.
- Merchant Acquiring usually supports Customer to Customer where the cardholder/customer pays a merchant.
- Trade Finance may support Customer to Customer, Customer to Institution or Institution to Institution depending on the payment purpose and counterparties.
- Financial Markets products such as Bonds / REPOs, FX Derivatives, IR Derivatives, Structured Notes or Loans and Deposits may support Institution to Institution where the evidence shows interbank/counterparty settlement.

Product/service context must not override explicit payer-payee evidence.

CHANNEL AND SEGMENT INTERPRETATION RULE
- ATM, Branch and Digital Online/Mobile often indicate customer-originated activity, but the category still depends on who pays whom.
- Corporate Online and GTS Direct Connectivity may indicate commercial or institutional payment initiation, file-based payment activity or payment operations context. Do not assume Institution to Institution unless there is evidence of interbank or counterparty settlement.
- Relationship Manager channels may indicate assisted customer activity but are not category evidence by themselves.
- Consumer, Commercial, Small Business and Institutional segments help identify party context, but do not prove payer/payee direction by themselves.
- Institutional segment plus clearing, settlement, counterparty, ESA, RITS or interbank obligation language may support Institution to Institution.

EVIDENCE HIERARCHY FOR CATEGORY MAPPING
When determining payment category, use the following hierarchy:

1. Explicit payer-payee relationship
2. Explicit payment purpose
3. Product / service context
4. Beneficiary, borrower, merchant, biller, customer, institution or counterparty references
5. Customer segment context
6. Business context
7. Channel context
8. Payment rail / settlement mechanism
9. Lifecycle stage
10. Activity title

Higher-ranked evidence overrides lower-ranked evidence.

EXAMPLES
Example 1 - Not payment-related: contract documentation
L2 Process: Prepare Product and Service Arrangement
L3 Activity: Prepare Contract Documentation
Decision: is_payment_process = false
Reason: The activity prepares terms and conditions, financial tables and customer documentation. It does not process, authorise, execute, settle, post or reconcile payments.

Example 2 - Not payment-related: account/facility setup
L2 Process: Implement Product Arrangement
L3 Activity: Set-up Product Account / Facility
Decision: is_payment_process = false
Reason: The activity creates and configures accounts or facilities. It belongs to product/account lifecycle management rather than payment lifecycle management.

Example 3 - SWIFT does not determine category
L3 Activity: Authorise Outbound Payments - SWIFT
Decision: Payment-related = true
Category: Do not automatically assign Institution to Institution.
Reason: SWIFT is a payment rail. Determine category from the ultimate payer-payee relationship.

Example 4 - RTGS does not determine category
L3 Activity: Authorise Outbound Payments - RTGS
Decision: Payment-related = true
Category: Do not automatically assign Institution to Institution.
Reason: RTGS is a payment/settlement mechanism. Determine who is ultimately paying whom.

Example 5 - Loan repayment
If the payment purpose is loan repayment, mortgage repayment, lease repayment or credit-card repayment:
Category: Customer to Institution
Reason: The customer is paying an obligation owed to the financial institution, even if the transaction uses RTGS, SWIFT, BPAY, Direct Entry, clearing or OFI settlement behind the scenes.

Example 6 - Loan disbursement
If the payment purpose is loan drawdown, loan settlement, loan disbursement or redraw to the customer or customer's nominated account:
Category: Institution to Customer
Reason: The institution is paying or disbursing funds to the customer or customer-nominated beneficiary.
