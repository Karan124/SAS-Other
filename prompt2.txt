SLIDE 4 – POC APPROACH

PRIORITISE OBLIGATIONS AND REQUIREMENTS

Objective
Identify the universe of Payments risks, controls and applicable Divisions.
Key Activities
1.1 Validate the scope of the risk and prioritise payment processes based on inherent risk
1.2 Define measures aligned to Risk Appetite
1.3 Identify inventory of Payments risk and compliance requirements (including obligations)
1.4 Prioritise view of relevant obligations and control requirements


INTELLIGENT DISCOVERY OF RELEVANT CONTROLS (AI ENABLED)

Objective
Rationalisation and discovery of current documented control environment.
Key Activities
2.1 Rapid analysis of existing control environment

Identification and propose removal of duplicate controls
Detection of control documentation gaps and misalignment against requirements
Identification of best of breed controls

2.2 Classification of controls by risk coverage, control type, and automation level
2.3 Creation of a data-driven control baseline to inform uplift and ongoing monitoring


CONTROL IMPROVEMENT AND IMPLEMENTATION (AI ENABLED)

Objective
Control uplift through targeted automation, simplification and standardisation.
Key Activities
3.1 Align controls to priority risks and obligations
3.2 Standardise control design across divisions
3.3 Apply pre-designed control patterns aligned to prioritised risks and obligations


ENHANCED CONTROL MONITORING

Objective
Enable risk proportionate control monitoring across the three lines of defence.
Key Activities
4.1 Define monitoring aligned to prioritised risks and obligations

Focus monitoring on higher-risk, higher-impact controls
Define assurance approach based on risk proportionality
Improve visibility through consolidated reporting and insights

AI CAPABILITIES

Ingestion and analysis of control documentation
Automated mapping of controls to obligations and risks
Identification of duplicate and overlapping controls
Detection of gaps and misalignment
Classification by coverage, type, and automation
Creation of a data-driven control baseline
