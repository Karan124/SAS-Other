A few examples of control descriptions:
Example 1
A monitoring and exception management platform is automatically updated from multiple source systems on a daily basis. The platform provides reporting and exception management capabilities for a range of monitoring rules reviewed periodically by the relevant business team.
Examples of rules include:

Incorrect pricing or rate application
Statementing exceptions
Inactive merchant/customer monitoring
Fee charging exceptions

Supporting documentation and dashboards are referenced via internal knowledge repositories.

Example 2
A billing and pricing request maker/checker process.
Requests are received through workflow tools and validated for completeness before processing in a billing platform. Where required information is missing, requests are returned to the requester for clarification.
Activities include:

Customer setup
Pricing configuration
Billing establishment
Independent quality checking
Exception reporting and investigation

Certain pricing changes require approval through an automated approval workflow before they can be finalised.
Weekly exception reporting is reviewed by relevant oversight teams.

Example 3
In the event an automation or robot fails to execute as designed, a manual fallback process is followed.
Activities include:

Receiving settlement data from multiple counterparties
Extracting and validating figures from source reports
Comparing results across systems
Independent checker review
Uploading settlement files to an external settlement platform
Performing reconciliations
Retaining evidence including spreadsheets, reports, screenshots and emails
