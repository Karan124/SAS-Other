Example 2: ORMB Billing and Non-standard Pricing request maker/checker   ORMB Business Support receive billing and Non-standard Pricing  requests via QuickService for actioning. The team validate the details that all the information are sufficient to process the request in ORMB. If required details are missing in the template then assign the request to the requestor for clarification. Noting the below clients of coverage:  1) Non-standard Pricing request: service Commercial, Business & Wealth clients only  2) Billing request: service WIB and Commercial, Business & Wealth clients  Non-standard pricing request received via QuickService or email must be processed prior to the billing establishment activities. The following actions are to be taken by WIBTO Ops: • Create a customer and assign a pricelist in the case of "new to bank" customer • Follow procedure for steps from create a deal (quote) for new to bank and existing customers to simulate a deal in ORMB • All non-standard pricing entered is automatically escalated to GTS Pricing for approval - GTS automated system Checker control (CTL000161769). Generally approved same day or within 24 hours. • Once the deal is approved in ORMB by GTS Pricing, WIBTO Ops proceed to finalise, accept and print a deal.   The following actions are taken for billing establishment activities:  • Action the request in ORMB based on the instruction Template sent via QuickService • Once completed actioning the request in ORMB then assign the request to Checker internally for Quality Check.   • Checker validate the details that all information are updated correctly in ORMB. If yes assign the request back to the requestor, if no then assign the request to the maker to amend the details.   • Once amended by the maker then the request will be assigned to checker. Checker reviews the request and assign the request back to the requestor.      ORMB weekly exceptions review   On a weekly basis, ORMB Business Support team generate an Exception report for Billing setups (usage, invoice and settlement constructs) from BI publisher report in ORMB and investigates new exceptions and follows up on existing outstanding exceptions.     If the investigation results in a required change in ORMB, the exception is entered into the Quality Check report before the changes are made in ORMB.  All changes are subject to a maker-checker process.    Weekly summary of exceptions reporting is provided to the GTS Pricing team for their review and CAS/Fulfilment Team Manager for oversight.

Example 3: In the event that the Robot malfunctions or fails to execute as designed, the below steps will be applicable at that failure point.  1. Nightshift Operations receive RBA ATM figures from three institutions - NAB, ANZ, Cashcard Network every day prior to 3.30am.  2. The Nightshift Officer obtains WBC figures from the ATM host report and inputs into the RBA pre-settlement spreadsheet. The Officer will also add the figures received from the three institutions.  3. The Officer compares the figures on the RBA Pre-settlement Spreadsheet with the "FSI by Counterparty report" in the RBA's Low Value Settlement System (LVSS). If figures agree, the Officer will save the data as .txt file  4. The Nightshift Supervisor/checker then manually checks that figures in LVSS match against the source data. 5. RBA.txt file is uploaded in LVSS prior to 4am.  6. Final figures will be sent by the RBA and appear in LVSS, which is checked by the Officer against the original FSI by Ctpty report.  7. The Officer emails the Spreadsheet, RBA.txt file, FSI screenshots (LVSS summary) and OFI emails to the Concord team as evidence.
