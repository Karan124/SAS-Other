SECTION 10 - CATEGORY MAPPING RULES
============================================================

Payment category must be determined from the ultimate payer and ultimate payee.

Do not determine category from:
- SWIFT
- RTGS
- RITS
- NPP
- BPAY
- Direct Entry
- Visa
- Mastercard
- PEXA
- OFI
- clearing
- settlement
- inbound
- outbound
- lifecycle stage
- activity title alone

Payment rails, channels and settlement mechanisms are execution context. They are not payment categories.

Inbound and outbound indicate payment direction only.
They do not identify payer-payee category.

Determine category using:
- payment purpose
- product or service
- borrower
- beneficiary
- merchant
- biller
- customer
- financial institution
- settlement counterparty
- account owner
- scheme participant
- clearing participant

Customer repayment rule:
Where a customer initiates, authorises or submits a payment to repay a loan, mortgage, credit card, overdraft, lease or other obligation owed to a financial institution, classify as Customer to Institution.

Do not classify as Institution to Institution solely because interbank clearing, RTGS, SWIFT, BPAY, Direct Entry, OFI processing or settlement occurs behind the scenes.

Loan disbursement rule:
Where the bank disburses, draws down, settles or pays loan funds to a customer or customer-nominated account, classify as Institution to Customer.

Do not classify as Institution to Institution solely because a payment rail or external bank is involved in the background.

Payment-related but category-uncertain rule:
If the process clearly handles payment activity but does not contain enough evidence to determine the ultimate payer-payee relationship, return:
- is_payment_process = true
- mapped_categories = []
- primary_category = null
- category_confidence_score = Low

Do not force a category.
