Question:

Should all linked activities be retained or should there be process selection logic?

=========================================================
TEST 2

Join:

l2_process_uuid
=
l2_process_UUID

Results:

36,000+ rows returned.

Reason:

One L2 process can have many L3 activities.

The join creates a many-to-many expansion.

This appears incorrect because controls become associated with every child L3 activity.
